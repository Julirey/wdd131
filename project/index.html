Did you know?

  • Mew was created only two weeks before the Pokémon Red and Green game development was finished. When the game was released in Japan, Mew was a secret character. Even Nintendo had no idea that Mew was in the game.
  • According to Snorlax's Pokédex entry, Snorlax isn't satisfied until it's eaten at least 900 pounds of food each day. Once it feels full, Snorlax goes directly to sleep.
